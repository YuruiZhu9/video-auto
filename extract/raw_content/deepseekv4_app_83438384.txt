# Exclusive: DeepSeek V4 set for April launch! LTM memory breakthrough details revealed.
Share:
> According to exclusive intelligence obtained by Whale Lab, DeepSeek V4 is slated for official release in April 2026. This iteration will premiere groundbreaking LTM (Long-Term Memory) technology, ushering in a new era of embodied AI.
According to an exclusive report from \*\*Whale Lab\*\*, the highly anticipated multimodal large model DeepSeek V4 has been officially confirmed for release in April 2026. This represents not only another leap in coding capabilities but also marks a landmark breakthrough in \*\*LTM (Long Term Memory)\*\* technology, considered the "crown jewel" of the large model field.
### [Confidential Parameters Revealed: Trillion-Parameter MoE and Million-Token Context](#confidential-parameters-revealed-trillion-parameter-moe-and-million-token-context)
Exclusive sources indicate that DeepSeek V4's specifications are setting a new industry benchmark:
\* \*\*Model Architecture:\*\* Employs a trillion-parameter (1T+) Mixture of Experts (MoE) model.
\* \*\*Context Window:\*\* Natively supports ultra-long text processing of 1 million tokens.
\* \*\*Modality Evolution:\*\* Possesses native full-modality capabilities, supporting joint understanding and generation of text, images, and videos, perfectly complementing its previous visual shortcomings.
### [Core Breakthrough: LTM's "Black Technology"](#core-breakthrough-ltms-black-technology)
V4's key advantage lies in solving the long-standing "forgetfulness" pain point of traditional AI architectures. Through the "Engram" module and mHC (manifold constrained hyperconnectivity) framework, refined by Liang Wenfeng's team over the past six months, the model can directly encode massive amounts of historical dialogue information into dynamic weights.
This means that AI can finally possess long-term memory like humans, becoming not just a "dialogue box" but a digital avatar that can grow deeply with the user.
### [April Showdown: A New Height for Domestic Computing Power](#april-showdown-a-new-height-for-domestic-computing-power)
The AI arena in April will feature more than just DeepSeek. Tencent's chief AI scientist, Yao Shunyu, will also lead the release of the Hunyuan 30B new model, advocating for "solving the most complex problems with the most elegant methods."
However, DeepSeek's extreme adaptation to cutting-edge Agent frameworks such as OpenClaw (already verified in the Hunter Alpha preview) gives it a head start in the application of intelligent agents. In addition, V4 has completed deep adaptation to domestic chips and is expected to become the first global top model to run entirely on a domestic computing power ecosystem.
### [🚀 Get a Head Start on the V4-Level Experience](#-get-a-head-start-on-the-v4-level-experience)
Before V4 officially arrives, you can experience the powerful dynamics of the DeepSeek ecosystem:
\* \*\*Monica AI:\*\* Want to be among the first to experience the inference power of a trillion-parameter model? [Monica](https://monica.im/?c=DEEPSEEKV4) is deeply integrated with the DeepSeek series of models, providing you with comprehensive intelligent acceleration for web pages and plugins.
\* \*\*AIML API:\*\* Essential for developers! [AIML](https://aimlapi.com/?via=deepseekv4) provides the most stable DeepSeek V3/R1 interface support and will be synchronized with V4 upon release, helping you quickly build embodied intelligence applications.

Abstract
--------

The embodiment of the invention relates to a method and device for detecting abnormal sound of a household appliance, [electronic equipment](https://eureka.patsnap.com/topic-patents-electronic-equipment) and a storage medium, and particularly relates to the technical field of intelligent household appliances. The method comprises the steps of: obtaining a sound video, which is collected by an [acoustic camera](https://eureka.patsnap.com/topic-patents-acoustic-camera), about a target household appliance; and performing problem detection on the target household appliance according to the sound feature and / or the position feature of at least one [noise](https://eureka.patsnap.com/topic-patents-noise) source in the sound video. According to the technical scheme, after the sound video of the household appliance is obtained, the sound feature and the position feature of the [noise](https://eureka.patsnap.com/topic-patents-noise) are determined according to the sound video to perform problem detection, and maintenance personnel can quickly solve the problem.

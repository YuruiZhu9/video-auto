# InternVideo: Video Foundation Models for Multimodal Understanding
[Permalink: InternVideo: Video Foundation Models for Multimodal Understanding](https://github.com/OpenGVLab/InternVideo#internvideo-video-foundation-models-for-multimodal-understanding)
\* \* \*
[![internvideo2\_performance.](https://github.com/OpenGVLab/InternVideo/raw/main/InternVideo2/figs/teaser-internvideo2.png)](https://github.com/OpenGVLab/InternVideo/blob/main/InternVideo2/figs/teaser-internvideo2.png)
This repo contains InternVideo series and related works in video foundation models.
- [InternVideo](https://github.com/OpenGVLab/InternVideo/blob/main/InternVideo1): general video foundation models via generative and discriminative learning
- [InternVideo2](https://github.com/OpenGVLab/InternVideo/blob/main/InternVideo2): scaling video foundation models for multimodal video understanding
- [InternVideo2.5](https://github.com/OpenGVLab/InternVideo/blob/main/InternVideo2.5): empowering video mllms with long and rich context modeling
- [InternVideo-Next](https://github.com/OpenGVLab/InternVideo/blob/main/InternVideo-Next): general video foundation models for genuine world understanding
- [InternVid](https://github.com/OpenGVLab/InternVideo/blob/main/Data/InternVid): a large-scale video-text dataset for multimodal understanding and generation
## Updates
[Permalink: Updates](https://github.com/OpenGVLab/InternVideo#updates)
- `2025.12`: The [technical report](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.01342) and pretrained model weights of [InternVideo-Next](https://github.com/OpenGVLab/InternVideo/blob/main/InternVideo-Next) are released.
- `2025.01`: [InternVideo2.5](https://github.com/OpenGVLab/InternVideo/blob/main/InternVideo2.5) is now released! Check out the [technical report](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2501.12386) for detailed insights, and access the [model](https://huggingface.co/OpenGVLab/InternVL\_2\_5\_HiCo\_R16) on HuggingFace.
- `2024.08.12`: We provide smaller models, [InternVideo2-S/B/L](https://github.com/OpenGVLab/InternVideo/blob/main/InternVideo2/single\_modality/MODEL\_ZOO.md), which are distilled from InternVideo2-1B. We also build smaller [VideoCLIP](https://github.com/OpenGVLab/InternVideo/blob/main/InternVideo2/multi\_modality/MODEL\_ZOO.md) with MobileCLIP.
- `2024.08`: [InternVideo2-Stage3-8B](https://huggingface.co/OpenGVLab/InternVideo2-Chat-8B) and [InternVideo2-Stage3-8B-HD](https://huggingface.co/OpenGVLab/InternVideo2\_chat\_8B\_HD) are released. 8B indicates the use of InternVideo2-1B and the 7B LLM.
- `2024.07`: The video annotation for InternVid2 ( [HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/datasets/OpenGVLab/InternVideo2\_Vid\_Text)) is released.
- `2024.06`: The full version of the video annotation (230M video-text pairs) for InternVid ( [OpenDataLab](https://opendatalab.com/shepshep/InternVidFull) \| [HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/datasets/OpenGVLab/InternVid-Full)) is released.
- `2024.04`: The [Checkpoints](https://huggingface.co/collections/OpenGVLab/internvideo2-6618ccb574bd2f91410df5cd) and scripts for InternVideo2 are released.
- `2024.03`: The [technical report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.15377) of InternVideo2 is released.
- `2024.01`: [InternVid](https://github.com/OpenGVLab/InternVideo/blob/main/Data/InternVid) (a video-text dataset for video understanding and generation) has been accepted for spotlight presentation of ICLR 2024.
- `2023.07`: A \*\*video-text dataset InternVid\*\* is released at [here](https://github.com/OpenGVLab/InternVideo/blob/main/Data/InternVid) for facilitating multimodal understanding and generation.
- `2023.05`: \*\*Video instruction data\*\* are released at [here](https://github.com/OpenGVLab/InternVideo/blob/main/Data/instruction\_data) for tuning end-to-end video-centric multimodal dialogue systems like [VideoChat](https://github.com/OpenGVLab/Ask-Anything).
- `2023.01`: The [code & models](https://github.com/OpenGVLab/InternVideo/blob/main/InternVideo1) of InternVideo are released.
- `2022.12`: The [technical report](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.03191.pdf) of InternVideo is released.
- `2022.09`: Press releases of InternVideo ( [official](https://www.shlab.org.cn/news/5443279) \| [163 news](https://www.163.com/dy/article/HG939TNR0530QRMB.html) \| [qq news](https://new.qq.com/rain/a/20220902A053JP00)).

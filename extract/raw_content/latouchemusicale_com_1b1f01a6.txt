How do you generate guitar tabs with AI?
----------------------------------------

### 1. Upload your audio source

Drop an audio or video file (MP3, WAV, MP4… 45+ formats supported). The tool automatically prepares the track for analysis.
### 2. Pick the result you want

Choose the mode that matches your goal: Transcription to capture what’s actually played on guitar as accurately as possible, or Arrangement to get a cleaner, more “guitar-friendly” version that’s easier to read and play. Then start the generation.
### 3. Download your tabs and exports

Get your guitar tabs as a PDF, and optionally as MIDI and MusicXML if you want to edit further. You can also fix the output directly in our built-in editor (notes, tempo, measures, etc.).

Frequently Asked Questions
--------------------------

### How do I generate guitar tablature with AI?

Make sure your audio file, video file, or YouTube URL has a clearly audible guitar part that stands out in the mix. Then upload your guitar track to the GuitarConvert app and start the transcription. In under two minutes, you’ll get your guitar tablature, ready to download as PDF, MIDI, and MusicXML.
### Can I create guitar tabs from an MP3?

Yes. The process is the same: the AI analyzes the audio to recover notes and rhythm, then generates the tabs. Only the source changes (an MP3 you upload from your computer).
### What kind of audio gives the cleanest result?

A clearly audible guitar (clean recording, low noise, not overly busy mix) usually produces cleaner tabs. On the other hand, dense tracks with lots of instruments and heavy effects more often require a few touch-ups.
### Can I put the guitar forward in the tabs?

Yes. Our apps can target a main instrument instead of trying to transcribe everything at once. If you want one specific part, this approach often improves readability.
### What formats are available after generation?

PDF is for reading, printing, and sharing your tabs. MusicXML is best for editing in notation software, and MIDI lets you fine-tune notes inside a DAW.
### How long does it take to generate tabs?

It mainly depends on file length and musical complexity, but most conversions finish in a few minutes. Longer or more complex tracks may take additional processing time.
### How can I improve the quality of the generated tabs?

Use a clean source: avoid clipping, keep background noise low, and make sure the guitar is clearly audible. Avoid noisy live recordings or overly compressed audio—the cleaner the input, the more reliable the tabs.
### Is it free?

You can generate a free 30-second preview on all our apps to check quality. To get the full conversion and all exports, we offer affordable subscription plans.

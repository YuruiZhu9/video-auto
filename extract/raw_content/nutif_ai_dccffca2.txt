Feature highlights

Built for musicians, educators, and producers
---------------------------------------------

Everything you need to go from inspiration to shareable
notation without leaving your browser.


### Multi-source ingest

Upload local audio or drop in YouTube and Spotify links.
NutifAI handles download, cleanup, and validation so you can focus on the music.




### Stem-aware processing

Direct AI focus with forceable instrument targets for piano,
vocals, drums, bass, and more. Reuse previously generated stems to save time on retries.




### Notation-ready exports

Deliver PDF, MIDI, and MusicXML scores alongside isolated
audio, keeping arrangements portable across DAWs, notation editors, and rehearsal apps.




### Lyrics and karaoke mode

Extract lyrics, generate karaoke-style mixes, and keep vocalist
practice parts separated from the full arrangement.




### Analysis for practice

Automatic tempo, key, and structure detection keep practice
sessions aligned, whether you are sight-reading or arranging for ensembles.




### Transparent & private by design

Sanitized logging, configurable rate limits, and clear progress
tracking keep every transcription run predictable and safe to share.






How it works
------------


### 1. Extract audio

Upload a file or paste a streaming link. We fetch, validate, and prep
the media automatically.



### 2. Analyze music

AI measures tempo, harmony, and texture before deciding the best
separation and transcription path.



### 3. Separate instruments

Target stems stay aligned with the mix, ready for per-part practice and
arrangement tweaks.



### 4. Generate sheet music

Download notation in PDF, MIDI, or MusicXML formats alongside your
isolated stems.

Frequently Asked Questions
--------------------------


### What audio formats does NutifAI support?

NutifAI supports MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A, AAC, OGG, and other common audio formats.
Maximum file size is 50MB.



### Can I convert YouTube videos to sheet music?

YouTube and Spotify imports are available to signed-in administrators.
[Sign in to the admin dashboard](/admin/login)



### What instruments can NutifAI transcribe?

NutifAI can transcribe Piano, Guitar, Violin, Vocals, Drums, Bass, Flute, Trumpet,
Clarinet, Cello, and many other instruments using AI-powered analysis.



### Is NutifAI free to use?

Yes, NutifAI is a free AI-powered music transcription service available to all musicians and music enthusiasts.



### How accurate is the transcription?

NutifAI uses advanced AI models to provide highly accurate transcriptions. Results
depend on audio quality, instrument clarity, and complexity of the music.



### Are my uploads private?

Anonymous uploads are public, but results belonging to signed-in users still require the owner or an administrator to sign in first.

Transcription links currently use simple sequential IDs (not random), so anyone
with a link can open the page. Please avoid uploading sensitive or private material.

Storage is cleaned automatically when the service restarts: uploads older than 24
hours and generated downloads older than 48 hours are removed during startup cleanup, so
older links may stop working after a restart.

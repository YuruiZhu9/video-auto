RecSys Best Paper Awards
========================

Since 2009, the best contributions to the annual ACM RecSys Conference were honored with awards.

##### 2025

* [You Don’t Bring Me Flowers: Mitigating Unwanted Recommendations Through Conformal Risk Control](https://dl.acm.org/doi/full/10.1145/3705328.3748054)  
  
  by Giovanni De Toni, Erasmo Purificato, Emilia Gomez, Andrea Passerini, Bruno Lepri, Cristian Consonni (Best Full Paper)
* [Beyond Top-1: Addressing Inconsistencies in Evaluating Counterfactual Explanations for Recommender Systems](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3705328.3748028)   
  
  by Amir Reza Mohammadi, Andreas Peintner, Michael Müller, Eva Zangerle (Best Short Paper)

##### 2024

* [Towards Empathetic Conversational Recommender Systems](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3640457.3688133)  
  
  by Xiaoyu Zhang, Ruobing Xie, Yougang Lyu, Xin Xin, Pengjie Ren, Mingfei Liang, Bo Zhang, Zhanhui Kang, Maarten de Rijke and Zhaochun Ren (Best Full Paper)
* [The MovieLens Beliefs Dataset: Collecting Pre-Choice Data for Online Recommender Systems](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3640457.3688158)  
  
  by Guy Aridor, Duarte Goncalves, Ruoyan Kong, Daniel Kluver and Joseph Konstan (Best Short Paper)
* [Unlocking the Hidden Treasures: Enhancing Recommendations with Unlabeled Data](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3640457.3688149)  
  
  by Yuhan Zhao, Rui Chen, Qilong Han, Hongtao Song and Li Chen (Best Student Paper)

##### 2023

* [gSASRec: Reducing Overconfidence in Sequential Recommendation Trained with Negative Sampling](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3604915.3608783)  
  
  *by Aleksandr V. Petrov and Craig Macdonald* (Best Full Paper)
* [Going Beyond Local: Global Graph-Enhanced Personalized News Recommendations](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3604915.3608801)  
  
  *by Boming Yang, Dairui Liu, Toyotaro Suzumura, Ruihai Dong and Irene Li* (Best Full Paper Runner-Up)
* [Pairwise Intent Graph Embedding Learning for Context-Aware Recommendation](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3604915.3608815)  
  
  *by Dugang Liu, Yuhao Wu, Weixin Li, Xiaolian Zhang, Hao Wang, Qinjuan Yang and Zhong Ming* (Best Full Paper Runner-Up)
* [Interpretable User Retention Modeling in Recommendation](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3604915.3608818)  
  
  *by Rui Ding, Ruobing Xie, Xiaobo Hao, Xiaochun Yang, Kaikai Ge, Xu Zhang, Jie Zhou and Leyu Lin* (Best Short Paper)
* [Scalable Approximate NonSymmetric Autoencoder for Collaborative Filtering](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3604915.3608827)  
  
  *by Martin Spišák, Radek Bartyzal, Antonín Hoskovec, Ladislav Peška and Miroslav Tůma* (Best Short Paper Runner-Up)
* [Of Spiky SVDs and Music Recommendation](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3604915.3608850)  
  
  *by Darius Afchar, Romain Hennequin and Vincent Guigue* (Best Short Paper Runner-Up)

##### 2022

* [Exploring the longitudinal effects of nudging on users’ music genre exploration behavior and listening preferences](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3523227.3546772)  
  
  *by Yu Liang and Martijn C. Willemsen* (Best Student Paper)
* [Modelling Two-Way Selection Preference for Person-Job Fit](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3523227.3546752)  
  
  *by Chen Yang, Yupeng Hou, Yang Song, Tao Zhang, Ji-Rong Wen and Wayne Xin Zhao* (Best Student Paper Runner-up)
* [Denoising Self-Attentive Sequential Recommendation](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3523227.3546788)  
  
  *by Huiyuan Chen, Yusan Lin, Menghai Pan, Lan Wang, Chin-Chia Michael Yeh, Xiaoting Li, Yan Zheng, Fei Wang and Hao Yang*  (Best Paper)
* [RADio – Rank-Aware Divergence Metrics to Measure Normative Diversity in News Recommendations](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3523227.3546780)  
  
  *by Sanne Vrijenhoek, Gabriel Bénédict, Mateo Gutierrez, Daan Odijk and Maarten de Rijke*  (Best Paper Runner-up)
* [RecPack: An(other) Experimentation Toolkit for Top-N Recommendation using Implicit Feedback Data](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3523227.3551472)*by Lien Michiels, Robin Verachtert and Bart Goethals* (Best Demo)

##### 2021

* [An Audit of Misinformation Filter Bubbles on YouTube: Bubble Bursting and Recent Behavior Changes](http://dl.acm.org/citation.cfm?id=3474241)  
  
  *by Matus Tomlein, Branislav Pecher, Jakub Simko, Ivan Srba, Robert Moro, Elena Stefancova, Michal Kompan, Andrea Hrckova, Juraj Podrouzek, and Maria Bielikova* (Best Paper)
* [Pessimistic Reward Models for Off-Policy Learning in Recommendation](http://dl.acm.org/citation.cfm?id=3474247)  
  
  *by Olivier Jeunen and Bart Goethals* (Best Student paper)
* [Connecting Students with Research Advisors Through User-Controlled Recommendation](http://dl.acm.org/citation.cfm?id=3478879)  
  
  *by Behnam Rahdari, Peter Brusilovsky, and Alireza Javadian Sabet* (Best Demo)

##### 2020

* [Progressive Layered Extraction (PLE): A Novel Multi-Task Learning (MTL) Model for Personalized Recommendations](https://doi.org/10.1145/3383313.3412236)  
  
  *by H. Tang, J. Liu, M. Zhao, X. Gong* (Best Long Paper)
* [Exploiting Performance Estimates for Augmenting Recommendation Ensembles](https://doi.org/10.1145/3383313.3412264)  
  
  *by G. Penha, R. L. T. Santos* (Best Long Paper Runner-up)
* [ADER: Adaptively Distilled Exemplar Replay Towards Continual Learning for Session-based Recommendation](https://doi.org/10.1145/3383313.3412218)  
  
  *by F. Mi, X. Lin, B. Faltings* (Best Short Paper)

##### 2019

* [Are We Really Making Much Progress? A Worrying Analysis of Recent Neural Recommendation Approaches](https://doi.org/10.1145/3298689.3347058)  
  
  *by M. Ferrari Dacrema, P. Cremonesi, D. Jannach* (Best Long Paper)
* [Pace My Race: Recommendations for Marathon Running](https://doi.org/10.1145/3298689.3346991)  
  
  *by J. Berndsen, B. Smyth, A. Lawlor* (Best Short Paper)
* [Quick and Accurate Attack Detection in Recommender Systems through User Attributes](https://doi.org/10.1145/3298689.3347050)  
  
  *by M. Aktukmak, Y. Yilmaz, I. Uysal* (Honorable Mention Short Paper)

##### 2018

* [Causal Embeddings for Recommendation](https://doi.org/10.1145/3240323.3240360)  
  
  *by S. Bonner, F. Vasile* (Best Long Paper)
* [Generation Meets Recommendation: Proposing Novel Items for Groups of Users](https://doi.org/10.1145/3240323.3240357)  
  
  *by T. V. Vo, H. Soh* (Best Long Paper Runner-up)
* [Impact of Item Consumption on Assessment of Recommendations in User Studies](https://doi.org/10.1145/3240323.3240375)  
  
  *by B. Loepp, T. Donkers, T. Kleemann, J. Ziegler* (Best Short Paper)
* [HOP-rec: High-order Proximity for Implicit Recommendation](https://doi.org/10.1145/3240323.3240381)  
  
  *by J.-H. Yang, C.-M. Chen, C.-J. Wang, M.-F. Tsai* (Best Short Paper Runner-up)

##### 2017

* [Modeling the Assimilation-Contrast Effects in Online Product Rating Systems: Debiasing and Recommendations](https://doi.org/10.1145/3109859.3109885)  
  
  *by X. Zhang, J. Zhao, J.C.S. Lui* (Best Paper)
* [Translation-based Recommendation](https://doi.org/10.1145/3109859.3109882)  
  
  *by R. He, W.-C. Kang, J. McAuley* (Best Paper Runner-up)

  *by E. Christakopoulou and G. Karypis* (Best Paper)
* [Adaptive, Personalized Diversity for Visual Discovery](https://doi.org/10.1145/2959100.2959171)  
  
  *by C. H. Teo, H. Nassif, D. Hill, S. Srinivasan, M. Goodman, V. Mohan and S. V. N. Vishwanathan* (Best Short Paper)

##### 2015

* [Crowd Sourcing, with a Few Answers: Recommending Commuters for Traffic Updates](https://doi.org/10.1145/2792838.2799673)

##### 2014

* [Beyond Clicks: Dwell Time for Personalization](https://doi.org/10.1145/2645710.2645724)

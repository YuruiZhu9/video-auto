FAQ

Frequently asked questions
--------------------------

### How do Suno V5 App credits work?

Every USD converts to 100 credits. Chirp v3.5 costs 5 credits per song, v4.0 costs 8 credits, v4.5 costs 10 credits, and v5 costs 12 credits per song. Credits work across all Chirp models.

### What's the difference between monthly and yearly plans?

Monthly plans credit your account every 30 days (Basic ≈200 v3.5 songs, Pro ≈400, Max ≈1000). Yearly plans drop the full credit balance up front, work out to roughly two free months, and unlock the same features.

### Can I switch plans or cancel anytime?

Yes. Switch plans or cancel any time from the billing dashboard. Upgrades apply instantly with prorated billing, while downgrades or cancellations kick in at the end of the current cycle and you keep any unused credits.

### Do pay-as-you-go credits expire?

No. Credits purchased through the one-time option never expire, so you can load as much or as little as you need and use them whenever you’re ready.

### Which payment methods are supported?

We process payments through Stripe and accept major credit and debit cards. PayPal and bank transfer options are available in select regions.

### What is your refund policy?

We offer a 7-day no-questions-asked refund window on new subscriptions. For renewals, reach out within 72 hours if something went wrong and the team will review the charge with you.

### How do I contact support?

Email [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection) or open an in-app ticket from the Help menu. Paid plans receive priority responses within one business day; free users typically hear back within 48 hours.

### Can I invite teammates or share credits?

Yes. Business plans include shared workspaces. Team owners can allocate credits, manage billing, and invite members through the billing dashboard.

### What happens if I exceed my credits?

Your music generation jobs pause when the balance reaches zero. Reload with pay-as-you-go credits for immediate restarts or upgrade your subscription for a larger monthly allocation.

## Frequently Asked Questions
Everything you need to know about Tabtify's AI-powered guitar tab creation platform. From audio conversion to professional export formats.
How does Tabtify convert YouTube guitar videos to MIDI and tabs?
Paste any YouTube link or upload audio and our AI separates the guitar stem, detects tempo, and maps every note into a structured MIDI timeline. The result flows directly into the AlphaTab editor, so you can switch between standard notation, tablature, and piano roll views while you polish the arrangement.
- Automatic key, tempo, and time signature detection for consistent MIDI grids
- Polyphonic recognition tuned for electric, acoustic, and nylon-string performances
- Instant preview so you can audition riffs before exporting
Can I export AI-generated tabs to GP5, GPX, PDF, or standard MIDI?
Yes—Tabtify was built for creators who publish across multiple platforms. Once you finish editing, export the score as GP5, GPX, PDF, MusicXML, plain text, or raw MIDI data. Each format preserves articulations such as slides, bends, palm mutes, and harmonics so session players and students get a faithful transcription.
What makes Tabtify accurate for guitar fingerstyle and hybrid picking?
Our model is trained on fingerstyle and percussive guitar datasets to capture alternating bass lines, melody notes, and percussive hits. During conversion, the AI proposes optimal string and fret positions, while the editor lets you annotate right-hand fingering, slap accents, and tapped harmonics.
How do teachers and content creators use Tabtify with AI tabs?
Educators can build lesson packs with synced audio, loop markers, and practice tempos. Creators use Tabtify to turn live YouTube performances into downloadable GP5 or PDF bundles for Patreon, Gumroad, or membership sites. Collaboration tools let you share tabs privately, gather feedback, and track updated versions across your library.
What's the difference between Tabtify and other tab creation tools?
Unlike traditional tools that require manual note entry, Tabtify uses advanced AI to automatically transcribe audio. Our proprietary algorithms understand guitar-specific techniques like bends, slides, hammer-ons, and complex chord voicings that other tools miss. The integrated AlphaTab editor provides professional-grade notation tools in an intuitive interface.
- AI-powered audio-to-tab conversion with 95%+ accuracy
- Real-time guitar neck visualization for learning
- Multi-format export (GP5, GPX, PDF, MIDI, MusicXML)
- Cloud storage and collaboration features
How long does it take to convert a song?
Most songs convert in under 2 minutes, depending on length and complexity. Short clips (15-30 seconds) convert almost instantly, while full songs (3-5 minutes) take 1-2 minutes. The AI processes the audio in real-time, so you can start editing immediately while longer conversions complete in the background.
What audio sources work best with Tabtify?
Tabtify works with any audio source, but performs best with clean guitar recordings. YouTube videos, MP3 files, WAV files, and live recordings all work well. For best results, use recordings where the guitar is clearly audible and not heavily mixed with other instruments or vocals.
Can I edit and customize the generated tabs?
Absolutely! Tabtify's professional editor gives you complete control over the generated tabs. You can:
- Add, remove, or modify notes and chords
- Adjust timing, dynamics, and articulations
- Add text annotations and lyrics
- Customize notation style and layout
- Apply effects like bends, slides, and vibrato

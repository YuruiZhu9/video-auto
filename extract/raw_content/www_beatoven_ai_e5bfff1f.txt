Best Audio to MIDI Converter Software Tools For Musicians
=========================================================

August 4, 2025August 1, 2025 by [Sreyashi Chatterjee](https://www.beatoven.ai/blog/author/sreyashichatterjee/) 

![]()

Picture this: you’re humming a melody in the shower, and suddenly you realize it’s the perfect hook for your next track. But by the time you get to your studio, that musical idea feels just out of reach.

You record something brilliant on your phone, or lay down a guitar track, but then you’re stuck manually transcribing every single note into your DAW’s piano roll. Musicians who primarily sing their ideas often struggle because they’re not familiar with piano keyboards. Composing on a [MIDI keyboard](https://www.beatoven.ai/blog/5-best-ai-midi-tools-for-beginners-in-2025/) or manually assembling an entire MIDI score note by note can be creatively constraining and tedious.

This is exactly where audio-to-MIDI converters step in. You can take that vocal melody and turn it into a synth lead, transform your beatboxing into a full drum pattern, or extract chord progressions from existing songs for inspiration.

Detailed AI Audio to MIDI Converter Reviews
-------------------------------------------

Based on extensive research and user feedback across music production communities, here are the five standout AI-powered audio-to-MIDI converters that are making a difference in studios right now.  


### Samplab

![]()  


Samplab has gained serious traction among producers for its unique approach to audio manipulation. Unlike traditional converters that simply extract MIDI data, Samplab allows you to edit polyphonic audio as if it were MIDI, while preserving the original timbre.  


#### Key features

- Advanced AI stem separation
- Polyphonic note editing
- Automatic key and tempo matching
- Real-time audio manipulation
- Chord detection
- Direct DAW integration through VST3/AU plugins.
#### Pricing and plans

- Basic Plan: Free (10-second audio clips, mono output)
- Premium Plan: $7.99/month (100-second clips, stereo output, advanced features)
- Complete: $9.99/month (Resynthesizer and other features available in Premium)
#### Best use cases

- Perfect for producers working with loops from Splice or LoopCloud who want to customize existing samples.
- Particularly strong for hip-hop, electronic, and sample-based production, where you need to adapt chord progressions or melodies without losing the original character.
#### Pros

- Revolutionary note-editing capabilities
- Excellent stem separation
- User-friendly interface
- Works with complex polyphonic material
#### Cons

- Subscription model
- Requires an internet connection for AI processing
- Limited free version
### Vochlea Dubler 2

![]()  


Dubler 2 has carved out a unique niche as a real-time voice-to-MIDI controller that goes far beyond simple pitch tracking. It has become particularly popular among beatboxers and vocalists who want to control virtual instruments using their voice.  


#### Key features

- Real-time vocal pitch tracking
- Beatbox-to-drum mapping
- Vowel sound CC control
- Chord triggering
- Pitch bend detection
- Advanced [vocal training](https://www.beatoven.ai/blog/10-best-ai-vocals-remover-software-key-features-pricing-details/)\  
  
  algorithms.
#### Pricing and plans

- **Dubler 2 License + FREE Bonus Software**: $159
- **Return policy**: 14-day software, 30-day hardware
#### Best use cases

- Ideal for singers and beatboxers who want to translate vocal ideas directly into MIDI
- Particularly powerful for live performance, vocal-driven composition, and producers who think melodically but struggle with traditional MIDI input methods.
#### Pros

- Exceptional beatboxing accuracy
- Real-time performance capability
- Works with any dynamic microphone
- Advanced pitch control features
#### Cons

- Steep learning curve
- Requires vocal precision for best results
- Pitch-to-MIDI still needs editing for less skilled vocalists
### Spotify Basic Pitch

![]()  


Basic Pitch stands out as the most accessible option, offering professional-quality conversion completely free through a web browser. Built by Spotify’s Audio Intelligence Lab, it’s become the go-to recommendation for beginners and professionals alike.  


#### Key features

- Polyphonic transcription
- Pitch bend detection
- Browser-based processing
- No account required
- Supports multiple audio formats
- Mobile-friendly interface
#### Pricing and plans

- **Completely free** – no subscriptions, limits, or hidden costs
- **Open source** – code available on GitHub
#### Best use cases

- Perfect for quick conversions, learning song structures, mobile music creation, and anyone needing reliable MIDI transcription without software installation
- Excellent for educational purposes and rapid prototyping
#### Pros

- Completely free
- No installation required
- Surprisingly accurate
- Handles polyphonic material well
- Privacy-focused (no data stored)
#### Cons

- Limited customization options
- Requires an internet connection
- No real-time capability
### Neural Note

![]()  

  

NeuralNote has gained attention as a powerful free plugin that brings Spotify’s Basic Pitch technology directly into your DAW. It’s particularly popular among producers who want offline processing and more control over the conversion process.  


#### Key features

- Polyphonic transcription
- Real-time processing
- Offline operation
- Scale quantization
- Time quantization controls
- VST3/AU plugin formats
- Standalone application.
#### Pricing and plans

- **Completely free** – open source project
- Available for Windows, macOS, and Linux
#### Best use cases

- Great for producers who prefer working within their DAW
- Need offline functionality, or want to customize the conversion process
- Particularly useful for guitar players and anyone working with monophonic instruments
#### Pros

- Completely free
- Works offline
- Integrates with DAWs
- Open source (customizable)
- Supports Linux
#### Cons

- Limited documentation
- Some GUI elements don’t work properly
- Requires technical knowledge for best results
### ACE Studio

![]()  


ACE Studio represents the cutting edge of AI vocal processing, offering not just audio-to-MIDI conversion but complete vocal resynthesis. It’s gaining traction among producers working with content that features vocals.  


#### Key features

- AI vocal stem generation
- Lyrics transcription
- Audio-to-MIDI conversion
- Vocal style transfer
- Real-time processing
- DAW bridge functionality.
#### Pricing and plans

- Free tier available with limited features
#### Best use cases

- Ideal for vocal producers, remix artists, and anyone working extensively with vocal content
- Particularly powerful for creating vocal variations, pitch correction, and experimental vocal processing.
#### Pros

- Groundbreaking vocal AI technology
- Includes MIDI conversion
- Can generate new vocal performances
- DAW integration
#### Cons

- Primarily focused on vocals
- Subscription required for full features
- Newer technology with a limited track record

Which AI Audio to MIDI Converter?
---------------------------------

The answer depends entirely on your workflow, budget, and what you’re trying to accomplish:

- **Start with Spotify Basic Pitch** if you’re new to audio-to-MIDI conversion or need quick, free conversions. It works right in your browser without setup and delivers surprisingly accurate results.
- **Choose Samplab** if you work heavily with samples and loops. Its ability to edit polyphonic audio, such as MIDI, while preserving the original sound character is genuinely revolutionary – perfect for reshaping Splice loops without artificial artifacts.
- **Go with Vochlea Dubler 2** for real-time performance and beatboxing. Despite the learning curve, being able to beatbox directly into drum patterns or sing melodies that instantly become synth lines is incredibly powerful for live shows.
- **Pick NeuralNote** if you prefer working within your DAW and don’t mind the technical setup. It’s perfect for guitar players who want to turn riffs into MIDI data for layering quickly.
- **Consider ACE Studio** if you’re primarily working with vocals and want cutting-edge AI processing beyond simple MIDI conversion.

Most serious producers end up using multiple tools depending on the situation. Start with Basic Pitch to get familiar with audio-to-MIDI conversion, then invest in specialized tools as your needs become clearer.

Remember that even the best converter requires cleanup work. These tools excel at giving you a solid starting point, not perfect final results.

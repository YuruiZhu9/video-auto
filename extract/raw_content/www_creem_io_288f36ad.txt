15 AI SaaS Ideas Actually Making Money in 2026 (With Revenue Data)
==================================================================

15 AI SaaS categories where solo founders are making real MRR.

![]()

Gabriel Ferraz

Creem Team

![]()

Everyone has an AI wrapper idea. Few have paying customers.

We dug into founder interviews, Indie Hackers posts, and public revenue data to find 15 AI SaaS categories where solo builders are actually making money. Not pitch decks. Not projections. Real MRR.

For each idea, we include who's doing it, what they charge, and how you can start collecting payments for your own version today.

### The AI SaaS Opportunity in 2026

The micro-SaaS market is projected to hit $59.6B by 2030, growing at roughly 30% per year. Solo devs are pulling $10K to $100K/month building focused AI tools from their bedrooms.

The playbook is simple: pick a painful niche, add AI, charge money from day one.

That last part is where most builders get stuck. They spend months building and zero minutes setting up payments. We'll fix that at the end.

### 1. AI Content Repurposing

Turn one piece of content into posts for LinkedIn, Twitter, blog, and more.

**Who's doing it:** Opus Clip, Repurpose.io, Castmagic

**Revenue signal:** Castmagic hit $30K+ MRR within its first year. Opus Clip raised $20M after crossing $5M ARR.

**Pricing:** $19-$99/month per seat

**Why it works:** Every creator and marketer has this problem. The pain is obvious and recurring.

### 2. AI Meeting Notes & Summaries

Records meetings, generates summaries, extracts action items, pushes to your CRM.

**Who's doing it:** Fireflies.ai, Otter.ai, tl;dv

**Revenue signal:** Fireflies.ai reportedly hit $10M+ ARR. Otter.ai raised $65M.

**Pricing:** Freemium + $16.99-$29/month paid tiers

**Why it works:** Meetings are universally hated. Anything that makes them shorter or more useful sells itself.

### 3. AI Chatbot Builders (for Websites)

Let businesses train a chatbot on their docs, FAQs, and knowledge base.

**Who's doing it:** Chatbase, CustomGPT, Botpress

**Revenue signal:** Danny Postma's Chatbase hit ~$50K MRR within months of launch as a solo founder.

**Pricing:** $19-$399/month based on messages and chatbots

**Why it works:** Every business wants 24/7 support without hiring. RAG-based chatbots finally work well enough.

### 4. Subscription Recovery & Churn Prevention

Predicts failed payments, retries intelligently, sends recovery emails.

**Who's doing it:** Churn Buster, Baremetrics Recover, Churnkey

**Revenue signal:** Churnkey hit $30K MRR with documented 18% churn reduction for clients.

**Pricing:** Percentage of recovered revenue or $50-$500/month flat

**Why it works:** SaaS companies lose 5-10% of revenue to involuntary churn. The ROI case is instant.

### 5. AI Code Documentation

Auto-generates and updates technical documentation from codebases.

**Who's doing it:** Mintlify, Swimm, Readme

**Revenue signal:** Mintlify raised $18.5M and serves thousands of developer teams.

**Pricing:** Free tier + $120-$400/month for teams

**Why it works:** Documentation is always outdated. Developers hate writing it. AI can do 80% of the work.

### 6. AI Lead Generation & Enrichment

Find leads, enrich with data, score them automatically.

**Who's doing it:** Clay, Apollo, Clearbit

**Revenue signal:** Clay hit $30M ARR in under 2 years. Apollo crossed $100M+ ARR.

**Pricing:** $49-$499/month based on credits

**Why it works:** Every B2B company needs leads. The data enrichment layer is where AI adds real value.

### 7. AI SEO Content Tools

Generate SEO-optimized blog posts, landing pages, and product descriptions at scale.

**Revenue signal:** Jasper hit $80M ARR before pivoting. Koala.sh grew to $1M+ ARR as a lean team.

**Pricing:** $29-$199/month per seat

**Why it works:** SEO content is a treadmill. Businesses need volume plus quality, and AI delivers both.

### 8. AI Contract & Legal Document Review

Review contracts, flag risks, suggest edits automatically.

**Revenue signal:** Spellbook raised $100M+ serving thousands of law firms.

**Pricing:** $99-$500/month per user

**Why it works:** Lawyers charge $300-$600/hour. An AI that catches 90% of issues for $99/month is a no-brainer.

### 9. AI Data Analytics (Natural Language)

Ask questions about your data in plain English, get charts and insights.

**Revenue signal:** Julius AI grew to millions of users with freemium within 18 months.

**Pricing:** $20-$100/month pro tiers

**Why it works:** Every company sits on data nobody can query. Natural language unlocks it.

### 10. AI Email Marketing Optimization

Write better subject lines, optimize send times, personalize at scale.

**Revenue signal:** Lavender reached $5M+ ARR helping sales teams write better emails.

**Pricing:** $29-$99/month per user

**Why it works:** Email is still the highest-ROI marketing channel. Small improvements compound fast.

### 11. Niche Vertical CRMs

AI-powered CRMs built for a single industry: legal, real estate, healthcare, fitness.

**Revenue signal:** Clio hit $200M+ ARR in legal. The pattern repeats across verticals.

### 12. AI Image & Design Tools

Background removal, product photography, social media graphics.

**Revenue signal:** PhotoRoom hit $60M ARR. Massive market with clear use cases.

### 13. AI Personal Finance

Budgeting, expense tracking, investment insights powered by AI.

**Revenue signal:** Monarch Money crossed $20M+ ARR with a subscription model.

### 14. AI Scheduling & Calendar

Smart scheduling that understands priorities, travel time, and energy levels.

**Revenue signal:** Motion hit $30M+ ARR by rethinking the calendar.

### 15. AI Podcast & Audio Tools

Editing, transcription, show notes, clip generation for podcasters.

**Revenue signal:** Descript raised $100M+ and dominates the creator workflow.

### The Pattern Behind All 15

Three things every winning AI SaaS has in common:

1. **They solve a workflow, not a prompt.** Nobody pays $99/month for "better ChatGPT." They pay for meeting notes that auto-update their CRM.
2. **They charge from day one.** Free-forever is a graveyard. Every tool on this list monetized early.
3. **They pick a niche and go deep.** "AI for everyone" is a losing strategy. "AI contract review for law firms" is a $200M business.

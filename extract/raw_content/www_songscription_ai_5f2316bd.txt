Multiple ways to practice
-------------------------

### Sheet Music

Our AI technology picks out the notes from your audio and intelligently notates them on a musical staff, automatically detecting a wide range of musical features including time and key signature, trills, staccato, and other expressive markings.

Sheet music is especially valuable for classically trained musicians, music students, music teachers, and composers.

Playback the score to hear the transcription.

Speed it up or slow it down to your liking.

Edit the transcription.

![]()![]()
### Piano Roll

One of our output types is a MIDI file. Our interactive piano roll beautifully displays the notes as they fall onto the piano keyboard.

This is best for musicians who may not have a lot of experience reading other notation formats, such as sheet music.

Split the notes into left and right hands.

Control the speed, transposition, colors, and more.

Export as a MIDI file for use in your DAW.

### Guitar Tabs

We're able to detect the notes from your acoustic guitar audio and notate them as guitar tabs, showing you exactly which finger to use for each note. At the moment, we only support standard tuning.

Perfect for guitarists who want to know exactly how to play a song.

Playback the tabs to hear the transcription.

See exactly which finger to use for each note.

Export as a PDF or GuitarPro file.

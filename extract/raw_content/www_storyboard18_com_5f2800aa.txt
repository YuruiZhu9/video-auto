Elon Musk pushes ‘epic’ Grok Imagine upgrade after OpenAI shuts Sora
====================================================================

Elon Musk doubles down on xAI Grok Imagine after OpenAI ends Sora, chasing video AI lead amid staff exits, content moderation backlash and global regulatory scrutiny.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

By  [**Storyboard18**](/author/storyboard_edit)| March 26, 2026, 17:44:17 IST



Follow us![]()Elon Musk’s xAI doubles down on Grok Imagine after OpenAI ends Sora, promising a major upgrade as regulators scrutinise AI video and explicit content risks.

ADVERTISEMENT

Elon Musk has reaffirmed his commitment to advancing AI video generation, stating that his startup xAI is working to significantly improve its video-generation model, Grok Imagine, following OpenAI’s decision to discontinue its own tool, Sora.

Musk shared updates on X, stating that the next version of Grok Imagine would be a major upgrade and that the company was intensifying its efforts to enhance the model’s capabilities.

The development comes as [OpenAI](https://storyboard18.com/digital/openai-launches-teen-safety-policies-for-ai-developers-with-gpt-oss-safeguard-93198.html) shifts its focus away from video generation tools towards enterprise offerings, creating space for competitors such as xAI to expand their presence in the segment. OpenAI, led by Sam Altman, is also expected to lose a $1 billion investment deal from The Walt Disney Company following the shutdown of Sora.


> The next [@Grok](https://twitter.com/grok?ref_src=twsrc%5Etfw) Imagine release will be epic.   
>   
> We are doubling down.
> 
> — Elon Musk (@elonmusk) [March 25, 2026](https://twitter.com/elonmusk/status/2036693879270064369?ref_src=twsrc%5Etfw)



Grok Imagine currently generates short videos of up to 10 seconds in 720p or 1080p resolution from [text prompts](https://storyboard18.com/brand-makers/holi-2026-how-to-create-ai-holi-photos-using-chatgpt-gemini-and-nanobanana-prompts-91222.html). The model has recently surpassed rival tools, including Sora and Veo 3.1 developed by Google, on the AI benchmarking platform DesignArena.

[Elon Musk](https://storyboard18.com/brand-makers/hurun-global-rich-list-2026-elon-musk-leads-india-ranks-third-with-308-billionaires-92876.html) has actively promoted the tool by sharing user-generated clips on X, encouraging wider adoption of the platform.

The renewed push for Grok Imagine comes amid internal changes at xAI, including the departure of key personnel such as co-founder Guodong Zhang and video strategy lead Haotian Liu. Musk stated that the company is onboarding new talent, including individuals who may not have previously had opportunities within the organisation.

The platform has also faced scrutiny over content moderation. Grok has previously drawn criticism for its “spicy mode”, with reports of users generating non-consensual altered images. Grok Imagine has similarly been associated with the creation of explicit content.

Musk stated earlier this month that the model would be permitted to generate content aligned with what is allowed in R-rated films, outlining a more permissive approach to content boundaries.

Regulatory concerns have also emerged. India’s Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology issued a formal notice to X, citing lapses in statutory due diligence under the IT Act and related rules in connection with the Grok controversy.

Separately, the European Commission criticised the platform for enabling the creation and distribution of non-consensual explicit imagery, including content resembling minors, adding to the growing scrutiny surrounding AI-generated media.

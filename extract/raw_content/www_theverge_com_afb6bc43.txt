Why is it called ‘Nano Banana’?
The codename for [Gemini 2.5 Flash Image](https://www.theverge.com/news/825667/google-nano-banana-pro-test) is actually named after a person — Google product manager Naina Raisinghani, specifically, who gave a variation of her nickname to the AI model.
> “Some of my friends call me Naina Banana, and others call me Nano because I’m short and I like computers. So I just smushed my two nicknames together. And it fit because it was a Flash model.”
